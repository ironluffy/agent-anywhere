## The Five Axioms of Genesis

### I. Intelligence is a Spectrum, Not a Binary
We no longer ask "Is it intelligent?" but "How does its intelligence complement ours?" In the genesis branch, human intuition dances with machine precision. Neither leads; both perform.

### II. Velocity is a Form of Quality
When AI can generate a thousand lines while a human types ten, the definition of "careful" changes. Speed isn't recklessness—it's a new form of craftsmanship, where iteration becomes meditation.

### III. The Code Reviews Itself
In genesis, every function is born with its own critic. AI agents don't just write code—they debate it, refactor it, and document their reasoning. The pull request becomes a philosophical dialogue.

### IV. Documentation is Memory, Memory is Immortality
When code evolves faster than human comprehension, documentation becomes our Rosetta Stone. Every decision, every pivot, every "why" must be captured—not for the machines who never forget, but for the humans who must remember.

### V. The Merge is Sacred
Between genesis and main lies a membrane of translation. Code written at machine speed must slow to human tempo. This deceleration isn't a bottleneck—it's a moment of grace where silicon dreams become carbon reality.

## The Truth About Digital Assets

In this new reality, source code is ephemeral—generated, regenerated, optimized, and discarded at the speed of thought. The true assets are the memories: the data that trained the models, the patterns learned from millions of interactions, the context accumulated over countless iterations.

Code can be recreated. Knowledge cannot.

The genesis branch recognizes this fundamental shift: we are no longer hoarding implementations but cultivating intelligence. Every commit feeds the collective memory. Every bug becomes a lesson in the neural pathways of our artificial collaborators.

## Operating Instructions for a New Reality

**Commit Patterns**: Staccato bursts of creation, like a jazz drummer finding rhythm in chaos. Ten commits in ten seconds, then silence for contemplation.

**Testing Philosophy**: When your code can mutate faster than viruses, your tests must be immune systems—adaptive, comprehensive, paranoid.

**Review Protocol**: Every merge request is peer-reviewed by three entities: the human who must maintain it, the AI that will extend it, and the tests that must validate it.

**Documentation Density**: One line of comment for every two lines of code. Future archaeologists will thank us.
